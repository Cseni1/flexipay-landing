What is FlexiPay?+

FlexiPay is an intelligent payment orchestration platform being built by FlexiWork. It brings payment methods and eligible routes into one experience, so senders and receivers can choose what works for them.

Can I use it today?+

We’re preparing early access. The first focus is USDC payments between compatible wallets on Solana. Request access below to tell us what you need; we’ll discuss availability with you. This website does not initiate payments.

Do both people need the same wallet or bank?+

That’s the friction we’re working to remove. The vision connects different funding and payout methods. Initially, wallet payments require compatible wallets and a supported asset and network. Other methods will become available route by route.

Will I be able to receive money into my bank?+

Bank payouts and digital-wallet payouts are part of the plan. Each route needs an approved partner, country and currency coverage, required identity checks and completed testing before it is enabled. We don’t currently promise bank payouts in a particular country.

How much will it cost?+

Our model is a fee per payment, with no subscription or minimum. The final quote will show FlexiPay’s fee alongside applicable network and partner costs before authorisation. Pricing varies by route; we don’t claim to be cheapest for every payment.

Is FlexiPay for businesses or individuals?+

Both. We’re building for personal payments, freelancers, businesses and platforms. Businesses can tell us about supplier, contractor and team payments; platforms can enquire about future API access and partnerships.

Is FlexiPay a bank?+

No. FlexiPay is a payment orchestration product in development, not a bank. Bank funding and payouts depend on eligible partner services. Wallet and digital-asset functionality is subject to availability and required checks.
