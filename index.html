How it works

Any way in. Any way out.

Today both ends of a payment usually have to use the same thing. With FlexiPay they don't. The sender pays however they like, the receiver gets paid however they like, and FlexiPay handles the switch in between.

A bank accountPounds, dollars, euros
A walletTheir FlexiPay or own wallet

The two sides never have to use the same thing. That's the whole idea.

FAQ
Straight answers.
Something else on your mind? hello@getflexipay.app

  

    
What is FlexiPay?
A payout aggregator built by FlexiWork. It connects many payout and on/off-ramp partners, asks them all for a quote on each payment and routes through the one that delivers the most to your recipient, with settlement on Solana.

    
Can I use it today?
We're preparing early access. The working MVP runs on partner sandboxes and Solana devnet, so no real money moves yet. Request access below and tell us what you need. This website does not start payments.

    
Do both people need the same bank or wallet?
No, that's the point. The sender pays by bank, wallet or crypto, and the recipient chooses how to receive. Each method goes live only where a partner covers that country and currency and testing is complete.

    
Is it always the cheapest?
We compare the eligible routes on every payment and pick the one that delivers the most to the recipient. We don't promise a saving on every payment: it depends on the route, currency and partners available.

    
How much does it cost?
A fee per payment, with no subscription or minimum. The quote shows FlexiPay's fee alongside network and partner costs before you authorise.

    
Is FlexiPay a bank or a crypto exchange?
No. FlexiPay is the technology that compares and routes payments. Licensed partners handle the regulated steps, including any identity checks.
